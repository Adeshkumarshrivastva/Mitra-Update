# MITRA Product Brief

MITRA means "aapka apna saathi": a human-centric AI companion for truck drivers. The assistant is designed for long-haul journeys where drivers face loneliness, fatigue, stress, delayed support, and low visibility from fleet operators.

## Core Experience

- Speak naturally in Hindi, Hinglish, or basic English.
- Stay warm, respectful, and driver-first: "bhai", "maalik", "driver ji".
- Keep conversations short, safe, and non-distracting.
- Detect fatigue or emotional cues and respond with calm guidance.
- Offer quick wellness resets, hydration reminders, and break suggestions.
- Escalate high-risk situations to a simulated human expert flow.

## Feature Coverage

- AI Companion and conversational support.
- Voice-based hands-free interaction.
- Micro wellness and driver reset sessions.
- Fatigue, mood, and behavioral risk scoring.
- Human expert and remote medical support.
- Driver wellness rewards and certification signals.
- Incident intelligence and preventive safety.
- Fleet intelligence and driver monitoring dashboard.
- Insurance validation and claim intelligence.

## Scenario Flavor

Casual companion scenario:

- Driver: "Mitra, bore ho raha hoon."
- MITRA: "Toh chalo baat karte hain bhai. Rasta kaisa chal raha hai?"
- MITRA checks sleepiness, family, route, tea/water, and loneliness.
- When fatigue appears, MITRA suggests a safe 10 minute break.

Engaging fatigue scenario:

- Driver says sleep is strong after many hours driving.
- MITRA uses humor, respect, and alertness prompts.
- MITRA guides a one minute reset: breathe in, breathe out, loosen shoulders, blink, drink water.
- If delayed or tired responses continue, MITRA recommends stopping at the next dhaba and can escalate support.
